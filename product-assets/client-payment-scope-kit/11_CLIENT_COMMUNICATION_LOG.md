# Client Communication Log

## Purpose

Use this log as the single source of truth for client-facing communication. Record important messages, decisions, promises, approvals, questions, and follow-ups on the same day. Copy the exact wording or link to the original conversation when it affects scope, payment, deadline, or acceptance.

## Payment Follow-up Log Entry

When following up on payment, record the invoice number, amount, due date, payment instructions, and the date of the next reminder. Never mark an invoice paid based only on a screenshot; use the payment verification process and retain the TxID or accounting evidence.

## Communication Quality Checklist

Before closing a conversation, confirm that the message identifies the project, states the requested action, includes a date or deadline, names the owner, and distinguishes approved scope from a new request. If a message changes money, schedule, or acceptance, create an Approval Record as well.
