# Filled Example — Small Business Website Refresh

This example shows how the blank forms work together. All names, dates, links, and amounts below are fictional.

## 1. Intake Form Summary

**Client:** Northstar Cleaning Co.  
**Primary contact:** Maya Chen, Owner  
**Business type:** Home services  
**Website:** `https://example.invalid/northstar-cleaning`  
**Main need:** A clearer service page and a working quote request path  
**Urgency:** Within 14 days  
**Budget range:** 50–150 USD  
**Decision-maker:** Maya Chen  
**Preferred contact:** Email

### Client’s situation

> Visitors can see the services but do not understand what to do next. The quote form is difficult to find on mobile, and the owner is spending time answering questions that should be answered on the service page.

### Desired outcome

> Make it easier for visitors to understand the service options and submit a quote request from a phone.

## 2. Proposal Summary

**Project investment:** 75 USDT  
**Estimated period:** 10 business days  
**Included revisions:** 2 consolidated rounds

### Included deliverables

| Deliverable | Quantity | Acceptance check |
|---|---:|---|
| Service-page content structure | 1 | Owner confirms the structure answers the agreed customer questions |
| Mobile-first service-page layout | 1 | Page displays correctly at the agreed mobile and desktop widths |
| Quote request form setup | 1 | Test submission reaches the agreed business email |
| Basic handover guide | 1 | Owner can update the listed text fields using the guide |

### Not included

New branding, paid advertising, copywriting for unrelated pages, CRM integration, and ongoing maintenance are not included.

## 3. Scope of Work

### Provider work

1. Review the supplied service information and current page.
2. Create one service-page structure.
3. Build one responsive page using the agreed platform.
4. Configure one quote request form.
5. Complete two consolidated revision rounds.
6. Test the page and form on the agreed devices.
7. Deliver the final link and handover guide.

### Client responsibilities

The client will supply the logo, service descriptions, contact email, business hours, service area, and consolidated feedback from one decision-maker within three business days of each review.

### Milestones and payments

| Milestone | Output | Payment |
|---|---|---:|
| Start | Project begins after deposit | 37.50 USDT |
| Review | First working page and form | Included in deposit milestone |
| Final | Accepted page and handover guide | 37.50 USDT |

## 4. Example Change Request

**Request:** Add a second quote form for commercial clients.  
**Why:** The client wants to separate residential and commercial requests.  
**Impact:** One additional form, two new routing rules, and extra testing.  
**Additional fee:** 15 USDT  
**Additional time:** 2 business days  
**Decision:** Approved by Maya Chen on `{{FICTIONAL_DATE}}`

The provider does not begin this extra work until the written approval and additional payment trigger are recorded.

## 7. Example Handover

The final delivery includes:

- The published service page link.
- The quote form link and a test result.
- The short update guide.
- The final accepted content file.

The client confirms that the files and links were received and that the agreed acceptance checks were completed.

## What This Example Demonstrates

The client is not asked to complete every document at the beginning. The intake form captures fit and context. The proposal creates a clear offer. The Scope of Work defines exactly what is included. The Change Request handles new work. The invoice links money to a clear trigger. The handover confirms what was delivered.
